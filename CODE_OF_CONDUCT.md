# Community conduct

Be direct about the work and respectful to the person doing it. Criticism is welcome; insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and spam are not.

Do not post someone else's private information. Do not use issues to organise attacks against a service or person. Keep disagreements about design and implementation on the work itself.

Report a problem privately to the repository owner where possible. Maintainers may remove content, lock discussions or restrict participation. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern.
